### What you get

- **Git to live pipeline**: push your repo and branch-based environments deploy automatically
- **Dev, staging and production**: isolated environments with repeatable promotion, approvals and one-click rollback
- **Safe backups and restore**: encrypted per-tenant backups with health monitoring
- **Real operational access**: terminal, code editor and SSH on the servers you own, not a sandboxed black box
- **Monitoring and alerts**: CPU, disk, memory and app-level signals, with alerts that catch problems before your users do
- **35+ one-click apps**: Grafana, pgAdmin, Prometheus, VS Code and more, with no bespoke infra work

Your cloud bill comes straight from your provider at their rates. There's no markup and no per-user fee.

### From signup to first deploy

1. **Sign up** and connect your cloud provider: Hetzner, DigitalOcean, or your own servers
2. **Provision a server** in one click, and CloudWady installs and configures everything
3. **Create a project**: link your Git repo, pick the Odoo version and edition, and set up your environments
4. **Deploy**, and every later `git push` deploys automatically
